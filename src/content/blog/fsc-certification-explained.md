FSC certification means that the paper or wood used in your packaging comes from responsibly managed forests, recycled sources, or verified controlled sources, and that an independent body has audited the supply chain against the standards of the Forest Stewardship Council. When sourcing custom packaging or paper products from Asia, finding an "FSC certified supplier" requires understanding exactly what the certification covers, what the labels mean, and what documentation you need.

This guide covers the meaning of FSC certified packaging, the three labels, how chain of custody works, how to verify a supplier in China or Southeast Asia, what the certification supports in compliance contexts, and the questions U.S. buyers ask most.

## What does FSC stand for?

FSC stands for the Forest Stewardship Council, an independent, non-profit organization that sets standards for responsible forest management and certifies companies along the supply chain that meet them. If you encountered "FSC" in a freight or shipping context, note that it is a different acronym in that industry — this page covers only the Forest Stewardship Council certification.

## What is the meaning of FSC Certified Packaging?

FSC certified packaging is packaging whose paper or wood fibre comes from responsibly managed forests, recycled sources, or verified controlled sources, and whose supply chain has been audited so that the FSC claim can be traced from the forest to your finished carton or bag.

One distinction matters more than any other: buying from a factory that holds an FSC certificate does not mean your specific product is certified. To carry the FSC label, your product's entire supply chain must maintain an unbroken **chain of custody** — and the claim must follow the product through the paperwork, not just the label.

## The three FSC labels

There are three FSC on-product labels, and they mean different things for your packaging specification:

1. **FSC 100%** — All the virgin material comes from responsibly managed, FSC-certified forests. Rare for standard commercial packaging.
2. **FSC Mix** — The product is made with a mixture of FSC-certified fibre, recycled material, and/or controlled virgin fibre (fibre verified as low-risk for illegal logging and other unacceptable practices). This is the most common label for custom cardboard and paper packaging.
3. **FSC Recycled** — The product is made from 100% reclaimed material. Unlike general "recycled" claims, which require no verification, the FSC Recycled label means the recycled content has been independently verified.

For FSC Mix, the certification body's control system determines what the claim is worth: under the percentage system, a product needs a minimum of 70% certified inputs to carry an on-product label. Below that threshold, the product can still be sold with an FSC claim on the invoice stating the actual percentage, but it cannot carry the label. Ask which control system your supplier uses — transfer, percentage or credit — because it affects what you can claim on your own packaging.

## How chain of custody certification works

Chain of custody certification is how the Forest Stewardship Council verifies that FSC material is tracked at every step from forest to finished product. A certified company must:

- Identify and track FSC material during manufacturing and distribution
- Keep all documents and records relating to FSC production, purchase and sales
- Meet FSC's core labour requirements (no child or forced labour, no discrimination, freedom of association)

Certification is issued by an independent, FSC-accredited certification body — never by FSC itself — and is maintained through annual audits. This is why a supplier's certificate has a holder, a scope and an expiry date, and why all three must be checked.

## How to verify an FSC certified supplier in Asia

Many factories claim to be "approved by FSC" or "FSC tested". Verify these claims during supplier qualification:

### 1. Request the Chain of Custody certificate

Ask the supplier for their FSC CoC certificate code (format: XXX-COC-123456) or their FSC trademark license code (format: FSC-C123456).

### 2. Verify the certificate online

Check the code on the official [FSC public database](https://info.fsc.org/). Ensure the certificate is:

- Currently valid (not expired or suspended)
- Issued to the exact factory name you are contracting with — not a trading company or a parent company
- Covering the specific product category you are buying (e.g., "Packaging and wrappings of paper")

### 3. Check the claim follows the paperwork

An FSC logo on the product is not enough. The invoice must explicitly state the FSC claim (e.g., "FSC Mix 70%") and the supplier's CoC certificate code, and the code and claim must match the supplier's certificate information in the FSC database. This invoice claim is what lets your own business keep the claim traceable through to your customers.

## FSC documentation for public tenders and compliance

If you need FSC documentation for public tenders or corporate compliance reporting, the requirements above apply with one addition: the FSC claim must appear on official sales invoices and delivery documents for every shipment, so the chain of custody stays unbroken through to you.

On labelling, FSC's own rules set the mandatory elements of an on-product label: the FSC logo and trademark symbol, the label title (100%, Mix or Recycled) and the FSC license code — with the web address and product type mandatory for products placed on the EU market from 27 September 2026. If you sell into the EU as well as the U.S., your packaging artwork must account for this.

## Does FSC certification help with EPR and retail sustainability requirements?

Partly — and the limits matter. Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) laws for packaging, now enacted in several U.S. states and in Europe, charge producers fees for end-of-life packaging management, often with lower fees for packaging with recycled content or higher recyclability. FSC certification does not by itself satisfy an EPR obligation — but certified recycled content and traceable fibre claims can support the documentation brands increasingly need when large retail partners or marketplaces ask for sustainability evidence. For a specific program's requirements, the program rules and your compliance adviser decide; certification is evidence, not a ruling.

## Common questions

**Why should I use FSC certified suppliers?**
Because the certification replaces unverifiable claims with audited ones. Any supplier can write "eco-friendly" on a quotation; an FSC chain of custody certificate is checked against a public database, audited annually by an independent body, and traceable through invoices. If sustainability matters to your customers or your retail partners, this is the claim that can survive scrutiny.

**How does a factory get FSC certified?**
The factory contacts an FSC-accredited certification body (never FSC directly), implements a chain-of-custody management system, passes an assessment and completes FSC Check before signing the trademark licence agreement, then maintains the certificate through annual audits. As a buyer you do not certify; you verify.

**What is the difference between FSC Mix and FSC Recycled for my boxes?**
FSC Mix allows a blend of certified, recycled and controlled fibre — standard for corrugated and paperboard. FSC Recycled requires 100% reclaimed material and usually carries a price or availability premium. Specify the label you want in the brief, and ask the supplier which control system they run so you know what claim your invoices can carry.

**Does an FSC certificate guarantee my product is certified?**
No. The certificate covers the holder's certified scope. Your product is FSC certified only if the claim is in your supplier's quotation and appears on the sales documents for your order.

FSC-certified paper and board is produced across China's packaging clusters, but certification claims are exactly the kind of specification detail that must be written into the brief and the quotation: the label, the claim percentage, the invoice requirements and the artwork placement.
