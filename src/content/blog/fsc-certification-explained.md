Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certification is increasingly requested by U.S. brands looking to improve the sustainability of their supply chain. When sourcing custom packaging or paper products from Asia, finding an "FSC certified supplier" requires understanding exactly what the certification covers and what documentation you need.

This guide explains the meaning of FSC certified packaging, the types of labels available, and how to qualify suppliers in China and Southeast Asia for public tenders or private corporate commitments.

## What is the meaning of FSC Certified Packaging?

FSC certification means that the paper or wood used in your packaging comes from responsibly managed forests that provide environmental, social, and economic benefits. 

However, simply buying from a factory that holds an FSC certificate does not mean your specific product is certified. To carry the FSC logo on your custom packaging, the entire supply chain must maintain an unbroken **Chain of Custody (CoC)**.

### The three FSC labels

When specifying packaging materials, you will encounter three main FSC categories:

1. **FSC 100%:** All materials come from responsibly managed, FSC-certified forests. (Rare for standard commercial packaging).
2. **FSC Recycled:** The product is made from 100% recycled materials (post-consumer and pre-consumer reclaimed materials).
3. **FSC Mix:** The product is made from a mixture of materials from FSC-certified forests, recycled materials, and/or FSC-controlled wood. This is the most common label for custom cardboard and paper packaging.

## Qualifying an FSC Certified Supplier in Asia

Many factories claim to be "approved by FSC" or "FSC tested". It is critical to verify these claims during your supplier qualification process in Asia. 

### 1. Request the Chain of Custody (CoC) certificate
Ask the supplier for their FSC CoC certificate code (format: XXX-COC-123456) or their FSC trademark license code (format: FSC-C123456).

### 2. Verify the certificate online
Always check the provided code on the official [FSC Public Search database](https://info.fsc.org/). Ensure the certificate is:
* Currently valid (not expired or suspended).
* Issued to the exact factory name you are contracting with (not a trading company or a parent company).
* Covering the specific product category you are buying (e.g., "Packaging and wrappings of paper").

### 3. Documentation for public tenders
If you need FSC certification documentation for public tenders or compliance reporting, you must ensure that your supplier includes the FSC claim on their official sales invoices and delivery documents. An FSC logo on the product is not enough; the invoice must explicitly state the FSC claim (e.g., "FSC Mix 70%") and the supplier's CoC certificate code.

## Sourcing Eco-Friendly Alternatives: Non-Woven Bags

While FSC covers paper and cardboard packaging, many brands looking for sustainable options also consider reusable **non-woven eco bags**. Non-woven polypropylene (PP) bags are durable, reusable, and often made from recycled plastics. They are a popular alternative to single-use paper or plastic bags for retail and events.

When sourcing non-woven bags from China, focus on:
* **Material thickness:** Expressed in GSM (grams per square meter). 80gsm to 100gsm is standard for retail.
* **Production method:** Heat-sealed bags are cheaper and faster to produce, while stitched bags offer higher durability and weight capacity.
* **Recycled content:** Ask suppliers if they offer non-woven fabric made from post-consumer recycled (PCR) materials, and request the relevant GRS (Global Recycled Standard) certification.

## Moving forward with your sourcing brief

Whether you are developing FSC certified paper packaging or non-woven eco bags, the requirements must be stated clearly in your initial sourcing brief.
