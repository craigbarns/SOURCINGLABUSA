Choose between Alibaba and a sourcing company by comparing who will define the product, qualify the supplier, check quotations, approve samples and follow production. Also identify who sells and invoices the goods. A marketplace is a way to find and transact with suppliers; a sourcing company's role depends on its contract. Neither route removes the need for a clear specification.

This is a decision framework, not a ranking or a review of individual suppliers. Platform terms change; check [Alibaba's own current information](https://www.alibaba.com/) and the exact transaction terms before relying on any platform protection. This guide does not promise that a badge, listing or payment method protects every order.

## Who owns each task in the two routes?

Use the table as a worksheet. Replace “confirm” with the actual person's or company's name. Buying through a marketplace does not stop you from appointing separate inspection or logistics providers. Likewise, hiring a sourcing company does not automatically include every service in its product price.

| Task | Direct supplier purchase | Sourcing or product supply arrangement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product brief | Your team prepares and agrees it with the supplier | Confirm who prepares it and who approves it |
| Candidate comparison | Your team gathers and evaluates evidence | Confirm the qualification work and its limits |
| Product invoice | Identify the seller in the transaction | Identify whether the partner is agent or seller |
| Sample approval | Your team records accepted features and exceptions | Your team still needs an approval decision |
| Production follow-up | Assign an internal owner or external provider | Confirm checkpoints included in the quote |
| Testing and inspection | Appoint providers when needed | Confirm scope, provider and separate charges |
| Import and delivery | Identify responsible parties and costs | Identify responsible parties and costs |
| Corrections or disputes | Review supplier contract and applicable platform terms | Review the relevant agency or supply contract |

The useful distinction is the work and commercial arrangement. An agent may charge for coordination while the buyer purchases directly. A product supply business buys and resells under its own agreed terms. Read [how these arrangements differ](/china-sourcing-agent) and ask for a written scope rather than inferring it from the label “sourcing company.”

## When can direct sourcing fit?

Direct sourcing can fit when your team can write an actionable brief, assess supplier evidence, manage comparisons, review samples and handle order follow-up. Use a named project owner and preserve the supplier's proposed deviations from the brief. The buyer should know which decisions require external technical or import advice.

It may be worth paying for specialist help at a particular stage even if the purchase remains direct. For example, product testing and a production checkpoint answer different questions; do not buy one assuming it covers the other. Use the [supplier evidence matrix](/blog/verify-chinese-supplier) to identify the gaps first.

## When can a product supply partner fit?

A partner can fit when a project involves several components, repeated specification discussions, sampling decisions or coordination that your team needs help managing. Evaluate the proposal against those tasks. Ask what you receive before ordering, which approvals remain yours, and how product pricing and additional costs are presented.

## A packaging comparison you can actually use

Imagine a U.S. brand needs a printed box, a fitted insert and a label. This is a hypothetical brief, not a client project. Quote A includes the box only; Quote B includes all three components and an assembled sample. Comparing unit prices immediately would hide the scope difference.

First, give both bidders the same component list and artwork revision. Second, ask for the same quantity and packing method. Third, list assembly, sample charges, tooling, transport and any excluded costs. Fourth, assign who resolves the fit of the insert around the product. Only then compare totals and the work still left to your team.

## Which questions should you ask before choosing?

Ask who contracts, who invoices, how proposed substitutions are approved, what the sample proves, which quality checks are included, and who owns unresolved shipment or product requirements. Request the answer in writing. If a proposal cannot answer those questions, record them as open items before making a purchase decision.
