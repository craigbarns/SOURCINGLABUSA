# Buyer acquisition takes priority

Owner clarification, 21 October 2026: the public website is first for prospective customers who want products sourced, manufactured and supplied. The offer for manufacturers preparing U.S. market entry is secondary. This clarification supersedes equal prominence for the two directions in the initial strategy and first implementation.

## Implemented hierarchy

- The English and Spanish homepage lead with the buyer's product, supplier options, samples, manufacturing and supply.
- The primary action is a sourcing quote. The secondary hero action explains the sourcing workflow.
- The hero product panel, primary service navigation, first project section and homepage tools all support the buying journey.
- No manufacturer market-entry card, readiness tool or manufacturer qualification link competes with the sourcing form on the main homepage.
- Default titles, descriptions, social preview, logo and company introduction emphasize sourcing and product supply.
- English and Spanish buyer homepages form the reciprocal `hreflang` cluster. `/zh` remains a separate Chinese manufacturer hub with its own language and canonical URL; it is not declared a translation of the buyer homepage. The WebSite schema still lists all three published languages.
- The resource, process and contact pages start with buyer preparation and the brief-to-order workflow.
- Existing market-entry, representation and manufacturer-tool URLs are preserved as secondary destinations. They are not presented as a second equal homepage audience.
- The current independent China partnership, deepest textile/packaging specialties, France/China contracting and planned U.S. expansion are stated where relevant without making the planned location the acquisition message.

## Acquisition priority

1. Qualified sourcing and product-supply inquiries.
2. Samples, comparable offers and orders from those inquiries.
3. Helpful buyer resources, especially the sourcing brief and landed-cost calculator.
4. Secondary manufacturer engagements through their dedicated pages, without taking over the main customer journey.

Measure the primary sourcing funnel separately. A future manufacturer campaign must not overwrite this confirmed homepage priority.

## Verification of the correction

Production build, lint, TypeScript and all 135 tests pass. The rendered-site audit passes 59 pages and 3,163 internal links with no broken links, duplicates or orphans. It explicitly verifies that EN/ES homepage metadata leads with buyers, the primary hero action reaches the sourcing form and the main homepage content does not promote manufacturer market-entry or sales-representation links. Desktop and 390×844 mobile previews were checked; the primary quote action reaches the sourcing form. No form submission or production deployment was performed.
