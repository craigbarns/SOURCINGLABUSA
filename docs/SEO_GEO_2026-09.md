# SEO and AI search implementation — September 7, 2026

This document supersedes the August audit where scope or implementation has changed. The U.S. launch remains planned for Miami in 2027. Clothing/textiles and packaging are core specialties; other products are assessed on request. The planned business purchases and supplies goods to business customers.

## Implemented

- Shared organization identity across English, Spanish, service pages and articles. No current Miami address, invented founding date, ratings, customer references or factory ownership in structured data.
- Unique page metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter descriptions; consistent production domain configuration.
- Reciprocal English/Spanish and x-default alternates in HTML and the sitemap. Only translated homepages declare language alternates.
- Organization, WebSite, WebPage, AboutPage, CollectionPage, BreadcrumbList, BlogPosting and visible FAQ structured data where appropriate. Homepage FAQ text and markup use the same source.
- Public content remains available in server-rendered HTML. Existing wildcard robots access covers search crawlers; the app remains excluded. Preview hosts carry a noindex response header.
- About page with factual business scope and planned commercial model. Homepage, category and guide links connect the content.
- RFQ guide with a downloadable, un-gated product brief template. Articles have section navigation, author links and visible update dates matching the schema. Existing URLs and original publication dates are preserved.
- Corrected outdated narrow positioning in the Miami article. Removed unsupported fixed fabric minimums, corrected care-symbol guidance, and replaced broad environmental claims with a practical sourcing checklist and official sources.
- Optional build-time Google and Bing site-verification tags, documented in `.env.example`.
- `npm run seo:audit` checks the production HTML of all 17 sitemap pages, 514 internal links, metadata uniqueness, canonical/social URLs, FAQ consistency, article dates, language alternates, resources and indexability. Run it against a local production server after building. The existing CI workflow is unchanged because the connected GitHub OAuth app does not have workflow-write scope.

## References and interpretation

[Google's guidance for AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) emphasizes crawl access, indexable text, internal links and structured data consistent with visible content. It does not require a special AI text file or special schema. Meeting technical requirements does not guarantee indexing or inclusion. This implementation applies those fundamentals to the actual business offer.

[Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) connect clear, discoverable content and sound indexing practices with eligibility for search and AI grounding. [Google's localized-page guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions) informs reciprocal alternates; [organization guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization) informs entity consistency.

FAQ markup describes the displayed questions; it is not a promise of FAQ rich results. The work contains no fabricated authority, keyword-volume estimates or guaranteed ranking/citation claims.
